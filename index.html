Mock Interview Practice Guide

  1. Resume: Upload your resume as a PDF. The AI will read it to understand your background and experience.
  2. Job Description: Paste the target job description. This tells the AI what skills and topics to focus on.
  3. Interview Options: Specify target company/role and choose the interview type (Behavioral, Technical, Product/Case, or Mixed).
  4. Webcam: Enable webcam if you'd like to practice with visual presence (this is optional).
  5. Practice: Click Start Mock Interview. The AI interviewer will introduce itself and ask a tailored question. Answer out loud!
  6. Dialogue: Respond naturally. The AI will listen, follow up dynamically, and guide you through a realistic 5-10 minute interview.
  7. Feedback: Click End Interview. The AI will generate a structured feedback dashboard with scores, strengths, weaknesses, and improved answers.
